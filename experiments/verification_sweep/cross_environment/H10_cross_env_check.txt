    The margin is 2.252e-05 Ha and the energy moved 1.005e-06 Ha -- 22x smaller than the
    margin. H10 survives the thinnest margin in the suite despite substantial package
    drift including a numpy major version.

    Compare C4H4_ccpvdz_PAR_HEA, which uses COBYLA: it moved 1.443e-02 Ha on the same

    Conclusion: optimiser family dominates margin. A thin margin is dangerous when the
    optimiser is gradient-free and largely harmless when it is gradient-based. The
    certification-margin heuristic should be read together with the optimiser, not alone.
